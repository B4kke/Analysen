# Roadmap

## M0 Foundation
Repo, containers, schema, configs, health, CI.

## M1 Norway Registry MVP
BRREG entities/roles/bulk role index/group structure + person/company lookup.

## M2 Evidence Graph
Raw snapshots, claims, provenance, conservative entity resolution.

## M3 Deep Web Research
SearXNG, crawler waterfall, archive/RDAP, leads.

## M4 Agentic Investigation
Planner/frontier/verifier/contradictions/checkpoints.

## M5 Financials/Documents
Annual accounts, normalization, formulas, multimodal fallback.

## M6 Product UI/Reports
Live research, graph, evidence viewer, report review/export.

## M7 Hardening
Norwegian eval suite, legal/privacy gates, auth/audit, security.

## M8 Monitoring
Delta investigations and source updates.

### Ikke før kjerne er stabil
Neo4j, mange ekstra OSINT-moduler, sosial-nettverksautomatisering, betalte dataleverandører, komplisert multi-agent framework.
